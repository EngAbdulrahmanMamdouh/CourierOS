# CourierOS - Project Context

# 1. Project Overview

CourierOS is a cloud-based SaaS platform designed to help courier and logistics companies manage their daily operations from a single integrated system.

The platform is being developed as a commercial software product that will be sold to multiple courier companies through monthly or yearly subscriptions.

This is NOT a graduation project.

The goal is to build a real business that can compete with existing courier management systems.

---

# 2. Vision

To build one of the best courier management SaaS platforms in the Arab region by providing a modern, scalable, secure, and easy-to-use system for courier companies of all sizes.

---

# 3. Business Model

CourierOS follows the SaaS (Software as a Service) model.

Every courier company creates its own account and subscribes to a plan.

Each company has completely isolated data and cannot access another company's information.

Revenue comes from monthly or yearly subscriptions.

---

# 7. Product Architecture

CourierOS consists of three applications:

1. Backend API

Provides all business logic and database operations.

2. Web Dashboard

Used by company administrators and employees.

3. Mobile Application

Used by drivers and customers.
